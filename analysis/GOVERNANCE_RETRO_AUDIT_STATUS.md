## Current governance findings

### 1. Raw event/out/base transition probabilities
Status: **BLOCKED for blanket production promotion**

The historical transition table contains 144 production-facing event × outs × base-mask states after bridging the current six-event engine. Raw state probabilities may only be used when sample support is adequate. The retro-audit uses a raw-state support diagnostic of n >= 385, corresponding to a worst-case Bernoulli 95% margin of error of approximately ±5 percentage points. States below that threshold are not permitted to enter production unsmoothed.

Required remedy: fit and validate an empirical hierarchical shrinkage rule using chronological development data. No fixed arbitrary pooling weight may be inserted.

### 2. Pitcher I1→I2 continuation
Status: **PENDING chronological benchmark result**

The retro-audit trains on 2021–2024 and evaluates on 2025, comparing the regularized logistic model with a constant training-base-rate benchmark. Promotion requires improvement on both Brier score and log loss.

Caveat: 2025 has already been viewed during prior model development, so it is not a pristine final holdout. Any passing 2025 result is development/validation evidence, not final proof. A frozen specification should be evaluated prospectively on subsequent unused 2026 observations.

### 3. Runner-transition temporal stability
Status: **WARNING**

`i2_play_calibration.json` aggregates 2021–2025 transitions. That artifact alone cannot establish season-by-season stability. The raw transition analysis remains useful as development evidence, but temporal stability cannot be claimed until event/out/base transitions are regenerated by season or from retained event-level Retrosheet data.

Required remedy: create season-level 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025 transition tables and test stability / chronological generalization before production promotion.
