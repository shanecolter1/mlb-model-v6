## Interpretation
- A residual present in both halves suggests a general inning/total structure rather than home/away asymmetry.
- A residual concentrated in only one half suggests team-role/home-away/run-allocation structure.
- This is a mechanism diagnostic, not evidence of sportsbook EV. Actual DK/FD inning prices remain the final test.
